Branding FAQs

Can you help an existing business refresh its brand?

Yes. Start by sharing what is changing in the business, current materials and where the updated identity will be used. We can discuss an appropriate scope.

Will branding help my advertising work better?

Clear identity and messaging can make advertising more consistent and easier to recognise. The exact creative and campaign approach is discussed for your business.

What should I prepare for a branding enquiry?

Bring your business name, services, audience, current logo or materials if any, examples you like and the places where you plan to use the brand.
