Brand campaign FAQs

What is included in a brand campaign enquiry?

We begin with your goal, audience, timing, key message and the types of media that may be relevant. The final scope, availability and commercial terms are confirmed separately.

Do I need to use every advertising channel?

No. A good campaign selects the channels that suit the objective and available budget. We discuss the relevant mix instead of treating every channel as compulsory.

Can a campaign focus on one Hassan District locality?

Yes. Let us know the town or market area, the audience you need and the reason for the campaign so the plan can start with that local context.
