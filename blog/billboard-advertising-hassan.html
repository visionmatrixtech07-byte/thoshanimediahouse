OUTDOOR ADVERTISING GUIDE

What makes a local billboard campaign easier to act on

Outdoor advertising works best when the location, short message and campaign period are considered together—not as three separate last-minute decisions.

A billboard is seen quickly, often while people are moving. This makes clarity more important than quantity. Before asking about a site or a quote, prepare the information that helps connect outdoor visibility to a real business purpose.

Decide who should see it and where

Start with the customer journey. Are you trying to reach people around Hassan town, a route toward Belur or Sakleshpur, a nearby local market or visitors approaching a business location? The desired route or locality is more useful than simply asking for “a big hoarding”.

Use one clear message

Outdoor advertising is not a brochure. A person should be able to identify the brand, understand the main offer and know the next action in a moment. Too many services, phone numbers or visual elements can weaken the message.

Know the period that matters

Link the campaign period to your objective. A festival offer, admissions window, new opening or event may need a clear beginning and end. An ongoing visibility goal may need a different duration. Include your preferred dates in the first enquiry.

Prepare artwork with the format in mind

  • Use a high-contrast brand mark and essential message.
  • Keep the primary offer short and readable.
  • Include only the contact or direction people truly need.
  • Confirm final artwork dimensions and specifications before production.
Remember: a requested location or date is not confirmed until the relevant availability, artwork requirements and commercial terms have been verified.
