Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Toshani Media House handles personal information you choose to provide through this website. It applies to the website contact and campaign enquiry flows, WhatsApp links, and the newsletter opt-in flow.

Information you choose to share

Depending on the enquiry, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, business name, campaign requirement, preferred dates, location or other information relevant to an advertising discussion.

How we use this information

  • To respond to your enquiry or campaign request.
  • To discuss services, availability, creative requirements or next steps.
  • To provide requested updates where you have opted in.
  • To maintain reasonable records connected to a business enquiry or agreed service.

Newsletter opt-in

The newsletter page creates a WhatsApp message that you review and send yourself. The website does not automatically submit or store the name and mobile number entered in that form. If you send the message, Toshani Media House may use the information in it to provide the updates you requested. You can ask us to stop at any time.

Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. We may share only information reasonably needed to deliver or coordinate an agreed service, or where required by law. Any media partner, payment provider or external platform involved in a separate service may handle information under its own privacy practices.

External links and embedded services

This website links to external services including WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn and an Amoggh TV live player provided through an external website. When you use these services, their own terms, cookies and privacy policies apply. We recommend reviewing them before sharing information.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected, business records or legal requirements. No online transmission or storage method is completely secure, but we take reasonable care appropriate to the information we hold.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements. You may also withdraw a marketing-update request at any time.

Please do not send sensitive personal, financial or identity information through a general website or WhatsApp enquiry unless we specifically ask for it through an appropriate process.
