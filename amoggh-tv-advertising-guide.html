TV ADVERTISING GUIDE

Preparing an Amoggh TV advertising slot request

A clear brief makes it easier to discuss a regional TV campaign. Before you request a slot, decide what the advertisement should communicate and when it needs to run.

Toshani Media House can collect a requirement for Amoggh TV advertising and coordinate an ad-slot request. The request itself is not a confirmed booking: scheduling, availability, creative specifications and commercial terms must be confirmed separately.

Start with the business objective

Decide what the campaign is meant to support. It may be a retail offer, a new opening, admissions, healthcare awareness, a property launch, an event or a brand-awareness campaign. One objective helps keep the video and call to action focused.

Prepare the essential details

  • Business or organisation name and a contact person
  • The offer, event or message to communicate
  • Preferred campaign start date and duration
  • The intended ad duration, if known
  • Whether approved video creative is already available

Plan the creative for a TV screen

Viewers need to recognise your business and message within a short time. The brand name, visual identity, offer and contact method should be legible. If your creative is not ready, explain that in the enquiry so the requirements can be discussed.

Be realistic about confirmation

A preferred date is an important request detail, but it is not a guaranteed slot. Toshani will coordinate the requirement; the final schedule is only confirmed after availability and relevant terms are agreed.

Good practice: do not publish a TV schedule as confirmed or make a related payment based only on an enquiry acknowledgement. Wait for clear confirmation of the arrangement.

Use the live player as a viewer, not an availability signal

The Amoggh TV live player on the Toshani Media House website lets visitors view the stream provided by the channel. A live stream does not show advertising availability; contact us with the actual campaign requirement.
